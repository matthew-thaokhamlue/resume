Personal project · Web product

Remarcable Living

A new city deserves a website that works.

Type Rental discovery website
Role Design and build
Timeline September 2026
Status Live at remarcableliving.co

Remarcable Living helps exchange students and interns find a Bangkok condo by starting with the neighbourhood, then the home. I took over a half-finished, broken build and turned it into a production-grade site: real pages, honest media, navigation that works, and a scroll hero that sets the mood. Live at remarcableliving.co.

Where it started

Unfinished, broken in production, and live anyway.

The site I inherited looked finished from a distance and fell apart on contact. The “Find by university” link returned a 404. The navigation did nothing in production because the client-side router was being answered with a redirect. Residence galleries fired requests for photos that were never committed. Every menu item pointed at an anchor on the homepage, and the hero spilled out of its frame on tablets.

Production-grade meant fixing the foundations, not just the surface. Every page got a real route, every image got an honest fallback, and every change shipped with a rendered-HTML test run and headless-browser evidence at phone, tablet, and desktop widths, driven through my Claude Code workflow.

What shipped

From a skyline to your own corner of it.

The homepage now opens on a scroll-driven hero: the Bangkok skyline zooms into a laptop screen, moves through a living room, bedroom, kitchen, and a river balcony, then hands off into the live residence collection under native scroll, with reduced-motion and slow-image fallbacks. Beneath it, a design-token system, shared navigation and footer, and per-page stylesheets replaced the tangle of one-off CSS.

The homepage fanned out into dedicated pages for residences, neighbourhoods, student housing, about, and contact, each with filters or prefills carried in the URL, plus a branded 404, a sitemap, and robots rules.

  • Navigation that works in production: full-page links replaced the swallowed client-side routes.
  • Real pages instead of homepage anchors, including the student-housing page that used to 404.
  • A build-time media manifest so missing photos render an honest note instead of broken tiles.
  • Tablet fixes: the hero stays inside its laptop frame and the nav collapses cleanly.
  • An About page with a permanent redirect from the old address, so shared links keep working.
