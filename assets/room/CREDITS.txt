The wood grain is drawn in room.js on a canvas; it uses no texture file. The trophies, the medals, the
certificates, the card tray, the business cards, the telephone, the radio and the music notes are built in room.js;
they use no model or image file. So are the decor: the bookshelf with its books, vases and basket, the trailing
plants, the tall plant with its broad leaves, the skirting boards and the wall clock. The floorboards, the rug, the
basket weave, the leaves and the clock face are drawn in room.js on canvases.
